Player guidebook draft

Dungeonlord

A practical field guide for building, raiding, surviving Councils, and keeping an endless dungeon alive.

How to use this document: This is written as a quick reference first, not a lore manual. Screenshot boxes are intentionally left blank so you can add your own images later.

Prepared for the current prototype build. Last edited: April 29, 2026.

Quick Start

Dungeonlord is an endless dungeon defense game. You build a dungeon route from the Entrance to the Core, staff monster rooms, choose invasions, survive raids, and use the rewards to expand before the next threat arrives.

First goal: Build a valid route from E Entrance to C Core, place at least one trap or monster room on that route, choose an invasion in Raid Forecast, then begin battle.
  1. Select an empty tile connected to your Entrance or route.
  2. Build a trap, monster room, or utility room from the Toolbox.
  3. Recruit or buy monsters, then place them into monster rooms.
  4. Pick Normal Hero Raid or Elite Expedition in Raid Forecast.
  5. Use Begin Battle, then Start Raid.
  6. Press End Turn until the raid ends.
  7. Spend rewards on more rooms, artifacts, monsters, dungeon upgrades, and evolutions.

The Main Screen

Center Grid

The dungeon grid is the main playfield. Rooms connect orthogonally. Utility rooms do not create walkable path.

Right Rail

Selected Tile and run actions live here. Check it when a room, trap, monster room, or Ash Breach behaves unexpectedly.

Menu

The hamburger menu opens Toolbox, Log, Inventory, Evolution, Glossary, and Council panels.

Dungeon HUD

Essence, Soulshards, Dominion, Core HP, Core Shield, build/battle mode, and dungeon validity appear above the grid.

Build Phase

Build phase is the planning layer. You can place rooms, staff monster rooms, buy from markets, evolve monsters, move rooms, upgrade the dungeon, and choose the next invasion.

Build Phase Checklist

  • Valid route: Entrance must connect to Core through path-carrying rooms.
  • Chosen invasion: Normal or Elite must be selected unless the day forces Escalation or Council.
  • Staffed monster rooms: Empty monster rooms do not defend themselves.
  • Armed traps: Trap readiness shows whether a trap has charges ready for the next hero entry.

Dungeon Level And Room Cap

Upgrading the dungeon increases your room cap. The current design expects a maximum dungeon level of 10, leaving unused tiles for labyrinth shape and temporary Ash Breaches.

Practical tip: Do not fill every tile just because you can. Empty space helps shape paths, makes Ash Breaches possible, and keeps the dungeon readable.

Battle Phase And Raids

Battle phase locks the dungeon layout and lets the invasion play out. Heroes enter, move through connected rooms, trigger traps, fight monsters, and try to damage the Core.

Core Buttons

Button Use
Begin Battle Leaves build phase after a valid invasion choice and valid dungeon route.
Start Raid Spawns the current invading party at the active entrance.
End Turn Advances hero movement, room combat, traps, statuses, and Core attacks.
Upgrade Dungeon Spends Essence to increase dungeon level and room cap during build phase.
Move Selected Starts build-phase room movement. Entrance cannot be moved.

Raid Types

Raid What It Means
Normal Hero Raid Baseline expedition. Usually the safest choice when testing a new layout.
Elite Expedition Smaller but stronger party with a leader trait. Higher reward and +1 Evolution on clear.
Escalation Raid Forced every fifth non-Council day. It scales upward as you clear escalations.
Council Retaliation Forced punishment raid from Dungeonlords, especially after repeated Council declines.

Rooms

Path-Carrying Rooms

Entrance, Core, trap rooms, monster rooms, and Ash Breaches count as path-carrying tiles. Heroes can move through them when connected.

Trap Rooms

Trap rooms trigger when heroes enter. They use charges and readiness. A trap showing R1 or higher is ready. A trap at R0 has spent its charges and waits for reset timing.

  • Traps can be armed, disarmed, and rearmed during build phase.
  • Some artifacts and linked rooms add trap charge or damage bonuses.
  • Trap kills can trigger economy effects from artifacts and support rooms.

Monster Rooms

Monster rooms hold defenders. The exact hero and monster count appears in Selected Tile and Tile Details. On the grid, green radar pings indicate monsters and red pings indicate heroes in monster rooms.

  • Use room staffing controls to place a chosen inventory monster.
  • Use Return to Inventory or Withdraw All to free room slots.
  • Training Den and similar permanent placement buffs stay with monsters after withdrawal.

Utility Rooms

Utility rooms are non-walkable support rooms. They provide auras or local effects but do not connect the route.

Common mistake: If the dungeon is invalid, check whether you accidentally tried to use a utility room as part of the path.

Room Links And Auras

Room Links and Auras are related but not the same.

Room Link

A new Blood, Ward, or Hunt room is linked if it is orthogonally adjacent to another new room with the same tag.

Aura

A utility room can affect nearby rooms within its stated range even if those rooms are not linked.

Example

Butcher's Shrine has the Blood tag. It links with adjacent Blood rooms such as Gore Channel or Carnage Pit. It does not link with Arrow Gallery, because Arrow Gallery is not a Blood room.

However, Butcher's Shrine can still appear in the Aura list for nearby rooms because its base utility effect affects hero deaths within 1 tile.

Tag Identity What To Look For
Blood Kill rewards, wounded heroes, healing, Essence and Soulshards. Gore Channel, Carnage Pit, Butcher's Shrine.
Ward Guard, defense, Weaken, trap charge support. Warding Sigil, Bulwark Hall, Aegis Lantern.
Hunt Lure, speed, Marked heroes, scouting pressure. Murder Holes, Pack Blind, Scent Beacon.

Economy

Resource Main Uses
Essence Build rooms, upgrade dungeon, buy some artifacts, buy or recruit monsters.
Soulshards Buy Shady Dealer artifacts and other soul economy upgrades.
Dominion Battle powers such as Pulse, Shield, Speed, and Strength.
Evolution Evolve or ascend monsters into stronger forms.
Darkcrystals Flesh Market purchases, sacrifices, fusion, and doctrine pressure.

Shady Dealer Artifacts

Standard artifacts are finite collectibles with unlock days and copy caps. When the catalog is exhausted, the dealer may have nothing new to offer.

Monster Trader

The trader offers recruitable monsters based on current day unlocks. Later runs unlock deeper monster pools.

Flesh Market And Fusion

The Flesh Market is opened by Council access, especially through Maltheron. It uses Darkcrystals and competes with other long-run investments.

Sacrifice

  • Only inventory monsters can be sacrificed.
  • Unique monsters cannot be sacrificed.
  • Sacrifice converts monsters into Darkcrystals.

Fusion

  • Fusion uses exactly 2 inventory monsters.
  • The primary monster determines the body and displayed base identity.
  • The secondary monster contributes a trait, passive, and stat bias.
  • Unique and already fused monsters are not valid ingredients in the current version.
  • The result is a fused Abomination-style monster with inherited star, stage, and up to 2 passives.

Council Of The Dungeonlords

Every tenth day is a Council day. Council days interrupt the normal invasion cadence. You can attend, decline, accept boons, accept quests, and manage relationships.

Favor

Action Favor Effect
Attend Council +1 with each attendee.
Decline Council -1 with each attendee.
Accept boon +2 with that sponsor.
Accept quest +1 with that sponsor.
Complete quest +2 with that sponsor.
Fail or expire quest -2 with that sponsor.

Favor drifts 1 step toward neutral whenever a new Council convenes. Rivalries cause a 1 point opposite shift with rival Dungeonlords.

Favor Bands

Band Access
Hostile Boons and quests locked.
Wary Boons available, quests locked.
Neutral Boons and standard quests available.
Favored Boons, standard quests, and hard quests available.
Allied Full access, plus improved banded rewards when applicable.

Nihaza And Ash Breaches

Matriarch Nihaza introduces temporary structural pressure. Her trial opens Ash Breaches on valid edge tiles near combat frontage.

Ash Trial Rules

  • Standard trial opens 1 Ash Breach.
  • Hard trial opens 2 Ash Breaches.
  • Every active entrance must stay connected to the Core.
  • Complete the required raids before the next Council to succeed.
  • Breaches collapse on success or failure.
Important: Ash Breaches are temporary entrances. If one is disconnected from the Core, the dungeon becomes invalid until you reconnect it or the trial collapses.

Dominion And Emergency Tools

Dominion powers are battle tools. They are strongest when used to prevent a raid from snowballing into Core damage.

Power Use Case
Pulse Damages all heroes before they act. Best when several weakened heroes are alive.
Shield Adds Core Shield. Best when heroes are close to the Core.
Speed Monsters act first this turn. Best in crowded monster rooms.
Strength Monsters gain ATK this turn. Best when defenders are already fighting.

Evolution

Monsters can evolve when they have enough personal and/or global Evolution points. Evolution improves stats and can alter combat identity.

  • Elite and Escalation clears can grant bonus Evolution.
  • Some Council sponsors reward Evolution directly.
  • Do not hoard Evolution too long if a key defender is falling behind.

Common Problems

Problem Likely Cause Fix
Begin Battle is disabled. No invasion selected, invalid route, or Council not resolved. Check Raid Forecast, dungeon validity, and Council state.
Dungeon is invalid. Core is disconnected, utility room is breaking path, or Ash Breach is disconnected. Use path preview and connect every entrance to Core.
A support room says unlinked. It is not adjacent to a same-tag Blood/Ward/Hunt room. Place a matching tag room orthogonally adjacent.
A room shows an aura but no link. Auras and links are separate systems. Read Selected Tile: Aura means nearby influence; Link means same-tag adjacency.
Heroes reach Core too easily. Route is too short or monster rooms are understaffed. Add detours, trap chains, and staffed monster rooms before the Core.
Long run has no dealer stock. You may have capped all eligible standard artifacts. Advance to later unlock days or wait for future catalog updates.

First Run Build Advice

Simple Safe Route

  1. Place one trap room between Entrance and Core.
  2. Place one monster room after the trap.
  3. Staff the monster room before battle.
  4. Choose Normal Hero Raid for the first fight.
  5. Use rewards to add a utility room near the combat cluster.

When To Pick Elite

Pick Elite when your route has at least one reliable trap, a staffed monster room, and enough Core HP or Core Shield to absorb mistakes. Elite rewards are better, but early elite leaders can punish weak layouts.

When To Upgrade Dungeon

Upgrade when you are close to room cap and your next build plan needs more tiles. Upgrading before you know what you want can waste Essence that might have bought monsters, artifacts, or traps.
